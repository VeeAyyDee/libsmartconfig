ESP-Touch v2 final supervisor observations, 2026-10-06

After the authorized guide-overlap repair, independent checks passed:
14,935 core assertions on 447 frozen public-sender vectors; 6,697 capture
assertions across 744 simulations; 1,508 new assertions across all 377 entry
points of three repeating lab streams. The new acquisition check failed on
the pre-repair library. One million core and one million malformed capture
probes passed ASAN/UBSan with leak detection disabled in this traced runtime.

Controlled two-board testing completed all six rounds: original/new receiver
for plaintext, AES security version 1 and AES security version 2. Each round
decoded the exact synthetic credentials and reserved data, associated with
the WPA2 lab AP, obtained DHCP, exchanged a UDP echo and delivered the correct
7-byte port-mark/receiver-MAC acknowledgement. Marks 0, 1 and 3 exercised ports
18266, 28266 and 48266. New AES modes used the actual target PSA wrapper.
No premature result appeared in the eight-second pre-transmission interval.
Each round began with empty test NVS and verified saved PHY calibration.
Both full original flash images were restored and verified after the run.

The first hardware trial failed to acquire on the new plaintext receiver;
the guide-overlap regression and fix preceded the successful second trial.
The failed trial, pre-repair host failure and exact build/source snapshots
remain on the supervisor side.

Channel observation: new plaintext/security1 capture events reported channel
6 with overhead 80. Security2 reported channel 10 with overhead 80, then
associated successfully on AP primary channel 6 in 40U mode. Cause is not
established. Describe lock.channel as the observed capture channel, not a
guaranteed AP primary channel; do not force association to it. No code change
is requested on the basis of this observation alone.

Limits: one synthetic ASCII credentials/reserved vector per mode, controlled
unencrypted non-QoS From-DS transmissions with extra synthetic length padding.
AES protects the provisioning payload independently of this radio envelope.
No actual phone or encrypted AP-forwarded interoperability claim, no complete
vendor API/ABI replacement, and no authentication claim for CBC/CRC/padding.
Existing vendor Wi-Fi/PHY still provide radio and association. Nothing was
published and no license was selected. Implementation may read this feedback
only, not supervisor-side sources, logs, binaries or research artifacts.
